How it works

From your idea to your fitting.

Whether you live around the corner in Sekhukhune or on the other side of the country, it starts with one message.

  1. Share your vision

    Send your occasion, date and any inspiration: a screenshot, a colour, a feeling. Pebetse will help shape it into a design.

  2. Measure & choose fabric

    Get measured in studio, or follow our measuring guide at home. Then pick your fabric and finishes.

  3. Quote & confirm

    You'll get a quote and timeline. A deposit secures your spot in the stitching queue.

  4. Stitched by hand

    Your piece is cut and sewn from scratch, with fittings along the way where possible.

  5. Collect or deliver

    Collect in Jane Furse, or ask about delivery to wherever your occasion is.

Tip: Big days book up fast, especially December and wedding season. Reach out as early as you can.

Measuring at home

Not near Limpopo? No problem.

Use a soft tape measure, wear fitted clothing and ask a friend to help. Keep the tape snug, not tight. Send these in centimetres:

  • BustAround the fullest part of your chest, tape level all the way round.
  • Under-bustDirectly under the bust, where a bra band sits.
  • WaistAround your natural waist, the narrowest part of your torso.
  • HipsAround the fullest part of your hips and seat.
  • Shoulder to waistFrom the top of the shoulder, over the bust, to your waist.
  • Full lengthFrom shoulder to where you want the hem to fall, in the shoes you'll wear.
  • Arm & sleeveAround the upper arm, and shoulder to wrist if your design has sleeves.
  • HeightBarefoot, standing straight.

Meet the maker

Pebetse, and a lot of happy stitches.

PB_Creationss started in 2016 with a sewing machine and one simple belief: that every person deserves to wear something nobody else has.

From a studio in Jane Furse, Limpopo, Pebetse designs and makes every piece by hand, blending South African tradition with bold, modern shapes. Feathers, pearls, sculpted shoulders and shweshwe have become her signature.

Nearly a decade on, a community of nearly 30,000 followers across TikTok, Facebook and Instagram watch each new creation come to life. Many of them are proud clients themselves.
